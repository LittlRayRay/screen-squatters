screen squatters

A window into your day for your housemates.

Saved in this browser only. Your AI key belongs in the desktop app.

Open Screen Squatters on your desktop first.

What gets shared?

Visible page text, title, and URL without query or fragment. Up to 24,000 characters; AI turns use at most 6,000. No screenshots or form field values are collected by this extension. Displayed text can still be private.

Following is optional and asks for website access. It shares the focused tab every five seconds while housemates are awake. Existing tabs may need a reload. Stop following here to turn it off.

During AI turns, including automatic hangout, shared text can be sent to Google Gemini. Desktop screenshot fallback is a separate, opt-in setting in the home app.
