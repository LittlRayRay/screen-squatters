What gets shared?

The focused, non-private page refreshes about once a second and after page changes, scrolling and media events. Updates continue while the squatters sleep. Non-private tab open/close/switch and navigation events are also sent, including background tabs.

Includes readable page/viewport text, title, cleaned URL, description, headings, links, reading position, visible target positions, media playback state and captions already exposed by the page/browser. No passwords, form field values, cookies, screenshots, raw HTML or audio recording. Displayed content can still be private.

The live feed goes only to the local desktop app. It doesn't make an AI request each second. During companion turns, context may be sent to Google Gemini. Pause here to stop updates and clear browser context and pending tab reactions. Private windows are excluded. Internal/protected page contents are never read; their tab events contain no title or URL.
